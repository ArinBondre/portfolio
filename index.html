Embedded Systems PCB Design 3D Printing

Low-Cost CO₂ Monitoring Device

A compact indoor air quality monitor built around an Arduino Nano and MQ135 gas sensor. The device continuously measures CO₂-related levels and communicates status through a three-stage LED system (green, yellow, red) with an acoustic alarm at critical thresholds.

The project evolved from breadboard prototype to a permanent soldered PCB with a custom-milled circuit board and 3D-printed enclosure — improving durability, portability, and everyday usability.

  • Threshold-based alerts: <1000, 1000–2000, >2000 ppm
  • Custom PCB fabrication & sensor calibration
  • 3D-printed housing for field use

Renewable Energy Arduino IoT / ESP32

Dual-Axis Solar Tracker with IoT Monitoring

A dual-axis solar tracking system that dynamically aligns a panel with the sun using four LDR sensors and servo motors controlled by Arduino. The design uses closed-loop feedback to maximize energy capture throughout the day.

Phase two adds an ESP32-based IoT module for real-time cloud monitoring of voltage, current, temperature, and light intensity — enabling remote dashboards, data logging, and performance optimization.

  • Phase 1: Dual-axis LDR + servo tracking (Arduino UNO)
  • Phase 2: ESP32 cloud monitoring (ThingSpeak / Arduino IoT Cloud)
  • 3D-printed structural frame & sensor integration
