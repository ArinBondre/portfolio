FIRST Robotics — Team 2429

I am part of the build team for FRC Team 2429, the La Cañada Engineering Club. Across three consecutive FIRST Robotics World Championship appearances, I have contributed to mechanical design and fabrication, including the robot's navigation and arm systems.

About FIRST

Learning through engineering and service

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) engages students in hands-on robotics challenges that combine engineering, teamwork, and problem-solving. FRC teams design, build, and compete with large robots while also working to make a positive impact in their communities.

Team 2429, the La Cañada Engineering Club, brings students together to learn through design, fabrication, competition, and outreach. My build-team work focuses on mechanical design and fabrication, with experience on the robot's navigation and arm subsystems.

  • FIRST Robotics World Championship — three consecutive appearances
  • Build team contributions to navigation and arm subsystems
  • Mechanical design and hands-on fabrication
